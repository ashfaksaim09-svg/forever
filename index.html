For You
A love story in four chapters

Chapter One

✦ First Impression ✦

The Moment I Saw You

♦

I still don't know how to explain what happened the first time I saw you — like no joke, for a second everything around me felt quieter, slower… almost like the world itself paused just to let me notice you. You didn't look real to me. You looked like something straight out of a dream — like an angel that accidentally ended up on earth. Beautiful doesn't even fit you. Pretty doesn't fit you. Stunning doesn't fit you. You're beyond all of that. You're angelic, breathtaking, unreal — like a heavenly gift somebody sent into this world just to remind people that perfection can actually exist. And the crazy part? You probably don't even realize how powerful your presence is. One look from you can make hearts skip, and mine? Mine never really recovered from that first moment.

✦ Chapter Two ✦

Her Beauty

♦

Can I be honest? Your beauty is lowkey dangerous. Your eyes? They're not just beautiful — they're addictive. The kind of eyes someone could get lost in for hours and still not want to leave. Your smile? That smile should be illegal, because it can literally change someone's whole mood in seconds. Your face is soft, elegant, feminine, and somehow perfect from every angle. You're not just "cute." You're not just "pretty." You're all of it at once — gorgeous, classy, powerful, stunning, queen energy, princess vibes, and that little confidence that makes you completely unforgettable. And the craziest part? Even with all that beauty on the outside — your vibe is what hits the hardest. Because your presence doesn't just make people look — it makes people remember.

♦

✦ Chapter Three ✦

Her Soul

♦

But if I'm being real — as beautiful as you are on the outside, what truly got me was the person you are inside. The way you talk, the way you laugh, the way you care, the way you can be soft one moment and strong the next — that's what made me fall deeper. There's something about your soul that feels peaceful. You have this energy that's impossible to fake — this warmth, this softness, this confidence that makes you stand out without even trying. In a world full of people trying to be noticed, you shine naturally. You don't force it. You just are that girl. The girl people admire, the girl people remember, the girl who walks into a room and changes the whole atmosphere. And me? I didn't just fall for your looks — I fell for your heart, your mind, your little habits, your laugh, your silence, your energy… basically everything that makes you, you.

♦

✦ Final Chapter ✦

Forever & Always

♦

When I think about the future — I don't picture random places or random moments anymore. Somehow I picture you. I picture late night talks, laughing over nothing, building dreams together, supporting each other when life gets hard, celebrating wins together, growing older together — and creating a life that actually means something. I picture looking at you years from now and still feeling the same butterflies I feel today.

Because if I'm being honest — you're not just someone I admire. You're not just someone I like. You're the kind of girl a man sees once in his life and knows — yeah, that's her. The one worth fighting for. The one worth building with. The one worth loving with everything.

♦

So after all these words, after all these feelings, after everything I've wanted to say — there's only one question left…

Will you marry me?
